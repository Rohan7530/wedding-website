How it all began

Our Story

Chapter I

A glance, not a meeting

We can't really say we met. We simply saw each other for the first time at a relative's wedding: two strangers in the same celebration, neither knowing it was the beginning of their own.

Chapter II

One message on Instagram

Some time later, she was the one who reached out first, with a message on Instagram. A small hello that changed everything.

Chapter III

And from there, it all started

One conversation became a thousand. And now, by the grace of Allah, it becomes a lifetime, and we would love for you to be there as it begins.
